## Positioning

Learning Studio is a Next.js and MDX learning platform for senior frontend interview preparation. It packages a large handbook into volumes, topic cards, batch revision packs, mock interview prompts, local reading progress, and JS/TS practice labs.

## Portfolio Angle

This project should show product thinking, content architecture, frontend system design, SEO readiness, and disciplined incremental delivery. It is not only an ebook; it is a structured preparation product with navigation, progress, practice, revision, and deployment workflows.

## Talking Points

- MDX-backed handbook chapters with adaptive volumes and topic navigation.
- Batch pages for revision sheets, cheat sheets, mock interviews, and PDF exports.
- Local reading progress based on active time and scroll depth.
- Separate JavaScript and TypeScript practice grounds.
- Custom-domain-ready SEO with canonical metadata, sitemap, robots, Open Graph, WebSite JSON-LD, and TechArticle JSON-LD.
- Incremental workflow where content is added in reviewable question batches.

## Suggested Case Study Framing

Problem: large interview preparation material becomes hard to navigate and maintain as one long document.

Solution: a structured Next.js learning platform with MDX content, metadata-driven volumes, topic cards, batch revision packs, progress tracking, and practice labs.

Result: a scalable senior frontend preparation product with 360+ questions and a workflow that can keep expanding without losing structure.
